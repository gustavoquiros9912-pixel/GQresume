Token Agency

UX Strategy, Branding & Marketing Design

At Token Agency, I led UX strategy across websites, digital products and customer-facing experiences. My role extended beyond product design into branding, marketing campaigns and graphic design, ensuring cohesive customer journeys across all touchpoints.

I directed user research, usability testing and journey mapping to identify pain points, while also guiding creative direction, ads and marketing assets to unify digital and brand experiences.

01 — the challenge

Unifying product and marketing experiences

  • Fragmented journeys. Websites, products and campaigns lacked cohesion.
  • Unclear pain points. Needed research and testing to identify user frustrations.
  • Brand consistency. Ads and marketing assets had to align with digital experiences.

The goal was to deliver integrated, research-driven and brand-consistent experiences.

  • Ensured cohesive customer experiences across touchpoints
