Token Agency

UX Strategy, Branding & Marketing Design

At Token Agency, I was the sole designer and creative lead on client projects in finance, education and industrial sectors. I covered the full range: websites and digital products, brand identity, campaigns, ads and graphic design.

Being the only designer meant I saw every touchpoint a client’s customers would see. My job was to use research to find where those journeys broke down, then make every piece, from the first ad to the final product screen, tell the same story.

01 — the challenge

Marketing and product, out of sync

  • Fragmented journeys. Clients often arrived with websites, products and campaigns that had been built separately and didn’t feel connected.
  • Unclear pain points. It wasn’t clear where customers were struggling without research and testing.
  • Brand drift. Ads and marketing assets didn’t always match the digital experience they led to.
  • Very different clients. Finance, education and industrial audiences each needed their own tone and priorities.

The goal: give each client one connected, research-backed and on-brand experience from first impression to product.

02 — my role

Sole designer and creative lead

I owned UX, branding and marketing design for client projects, from research to final assets.

  • Led UX strategy for websites and digital products
  • Ran user research, usability testing and journey mapping
  • Set creative direction and brand identity
  • Designed campaigns, ads and graphic assets
  • Kept the customer experience consistent across every touchpoint

03 — approach

Three decisions that shaped the work

Because one person designed the ad, the landing page and the product, I could treat them as one journey.

01

Start with research

User-centered insights

Decision. Early in each project, I used usability testing and journey mapping to find where customers struggled, then iterated on what we learned.

Why. Research pointed the work at real problems instead of assumptions.

02

One brand, from ad to product

Creative alignment

Decision. I applied the same brand language across ads, campaigns, websites and digital products, so marketing and product looked and sounded like one company.

Why. When an ad and the site it leads to don’t match, the experience feels disconnected.

03

Design the whole journey

Experience cohesion

Decision. I designed flows that connected campaigns to websites and products, and improved usability at each step instead of polishing pieces in isolation.

Why. Customers experience a brand as one path, not a set of separate deliverables.

05 — reflection

What I took from it

  • UX and marketing are the same conversation. Treating the ad and the product as two halves of one experience is what made the work feel cohesive.
  • Research keeps design honest. Testing and journey maps point the work at real problems.
  • Range is a skill. Moving between finance, education and industrial clients taught me to adapt tone and priorities without losing craft.
