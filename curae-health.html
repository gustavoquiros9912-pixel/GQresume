Curae Health

Healthcare Financial Platform & Curae Product Experience

Curae Health is a healthcare financial technology company that provides liquidity, payment acceleration and revenue solutions to healthcare organizations and their clients. Its core product, Curae, sits under the Curae Health parent brand.

As the sole senior UI/UX and graphic designer, I design the whole experience: the marketing sites that explain the offering, the portal clients work in, the billing and payment flows patients see, and the sales materials that support all of it.

01 — the challenge

Complex finance, high stakes

  • Hard-to-explain products. Healthcare payments, liquidity and working capital are hard concepts, even for the people buying them.
  • Two brands, one journey. Curae Health and Curae serve different stages of the customer journey but had to feel like one company.
  • Money and health together. Whether it’s a healthcare organization managing revenue or a patient paying a bill, people need to trust what they see before they act.
  • One designer. Every surface, from the website to the portal to a sales one-pager, came through the same person, so consistency had to be designed in, not policed.

The goal: make healthcare finance understandable, approachable and trustworthy at every touchpoint.

03 — approach

Three decisions that shaped the work

Each audience needed something different: buyers needed to understand the value, clients needed to get work done and patients needed to pay with confidence. The work was making one experience serve all three.

01

Lead with value, reveal the detail

User-centered communication

Decision. Pages open with a clear value proposition and are built around real use cases. The financial mechanics come next, revealed step by step instead of all at once.

Why. Visitors need to see quickly whether Curae is relevant to them. The detail matters once they’re interested.

02

One parent, one product, shared parts

Scalability

Decision. I positioned Curae Health as the parent platform and Curae as the core product, and built both from the same layouts and components.

Result. The two brands read as one company, and new pages and features start from existing pieces instead of a blank canvas.

03

Design for confidence

Trust through consistency

Decision. A clean, predictable visual hierarchy, plain language instead of financial jargon, and interactions that behave the same everywhere.

Where it matters most. In billing and payments, where people need to know exactly what they owe, what they’re paying and what happens next.

05 — reflection

What I took from it

  • Clarity, consistency and trust depend on each other. In healthcare finance, a confusing screen doesn’t just slow people down. It makes them doubt the numbers.
  • Being the only designer means designing the system first. Shared layouts and components are what let one person keep four surfaces consistent.
  • The same story has to hold everywhere. When the sales deck, the website and the payment screen say the same thing the same way, people trust all three more.
