Principles

  • Dark on purpose.The black is the brand, so there is no light mode.
  • One accent.Salmon marks what matters. It never decorates.
  • Space and hairlines, not boxes.Content is grouped by rhythm and thin rules.
  • Proof over claims.Pages show decisions and evidence, not adjectives.
  • One radius.4px on every surface and control.
  • Motion earns its place.Reveal hierarchy or show progress. Stop for reduced-motion visitors.
