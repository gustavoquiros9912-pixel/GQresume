Paper and pixels

The desk from the film, made twice: once in cut paper, once in pixel art. Drag the line to compare them.

Paper Pixels

Cut paper

Every piece is vector, cut with edges that are never quite straight, lit by two shadows and a paper grain. It moves like stop-motion, about twelve drawings a second.

Pixel art

The same room at 192 by 108 pixels, drawn from one sprite sheet and only ever scaled by whole numbers. Both versions run the same loop: the line is typed, then the button is pressed.
